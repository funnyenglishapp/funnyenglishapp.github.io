Privacy Policy

Last updated: June, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our application, FunnyEnglish, handles your information and protects your privacy. By using our application, you signify your agreement to the practices described in this comprehensive policy. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection and transparency.

1. Service Architecture & Notifications

To ensure our users always have access to the most current educational content and timely updates, our application utilizes secure, industry-standard service endpoints. These systems facilitate internal database synchronization and support essential service notifications to keep you informed about important app improvements.

2. Interactive Audio Analysis

A core feature of our application is the 'Voice Game,' which provides real-time feedback on English pronunciation using privacy-first, on-device native technologies. Audio data is processed in real-time and is never stored or transmitted externally.

3. Data Collection & Privacy Principles

We operate on a foundational principle of Zero-Personal Data Collection. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information such as your name, email, or physical location.

4. Localized Information & Data Encryption

All preferences, learning progress, and educational alerts are managed locally on your device. For enhanced protection, sensitive internal configuration data is secured using hardware-backed encryption methods to ensure consistency without requiring external tracking.

5. System Security & Integrity

To protect our users and maintain the integrity of the educational experience, the application runs in a secure sandbox environment and employs automated fraud prevention and system security measures to detect unauthorized tampering or security breaches.

6. Children’s Privacy Compliance

No personal data is collected from any users, including children. The application is designed to be a safe educational environment for everyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect security enhancements. Any changes will be updated on this page with a new 'Last Updated' date.
